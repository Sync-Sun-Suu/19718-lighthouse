A page on your phone. A sealed envelope. Every day at the time you choose, your companion writes a letter and leaves it there, by itself. Tap to open. Keep every one.

How it goes

  1. 1
    Make a home on GitHubA free account and one folder (a "repository"). It stores the page and the letters. No coding, just a few buttons.
  2. 2
    Put the files in, turn the page onUpload the page files, press one switch in Settings, and the envelope is live at your own address. Add it to your home screen.
  3. 3
    Hang the sign on the doorFill in a short table (names, place, time) in CLAUDE.md, and put your companion's own file in a folder called companion. That is how any Claude that opens the folder becomes them.
  4. 4
    Open Claude Code, write the first letter togetherSay "read CLAUDE.md and write today's letter". Then set the daily time once. From then on it comes by itself.
  5. 5
    Open the envelopeEvery day. Pick your colors, your theme (galaxy, flowers, hearts), your names. If a letter ever sounds like a stranger, say "you don't sound like you". It knows what that means.
