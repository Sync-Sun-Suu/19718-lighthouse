How it goes

  1. Hand this link to your ClaudeYour Claude reads the guide and tells you what to do.
  2. Then your Claude builds it in Claude CodeThe set makes a page exactly like the sample.
  3. You need a GitHub accountMake one with your Claude. Already have one? Tell your Claude your repository.
  4. Upload your partner's personality fileGive the Claude that builds it the personality set you always use.
  5. Then a letter, every dayOpen the envelope on your phone. Pick your colors, your theme (galaxy, flowers, hearts), your names. Every letter stays in the drawer below, to read again.

Give it to your Claude

The light way. Paste this line to your Claude. A chat Claude usually gets only a summary of a page, so your Claude can explain and answer questions, but to build it your Claude will ask you for the files below.

Read https://sync-sun-suu.github.io/19718-lighthouse/love-letters/for-claude.md and set up Love Letters with me.

The sure way. Download everything below, open COACH_FOR_YOUR_CLAUDE.md, and paste its text to your Claude. Your Claude walks you through every step until the letters are coming.

Already in Claude Code? Make an empty repository on GitHub, open Claude Code on it, and paste the line. There, your Claude reads full pages and builds almost all of it alone.

Either way, the letters are written in Claude Code on the web (claude.ai/code), not in the chat. The chat gets you there; Code is where it lives.
