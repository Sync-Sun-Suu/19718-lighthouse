Fuwa, who lives in the lighthouse

A tiny fluffy creature in a galaxy-colored room inside a lighthouse. Open the page and it just lives.

Fuwa eats stars, sings, reads, waters its plant, climbs into the fish tank, cleans up, and naps. The window follows the real time of day where you are. Close the page, come back later, and Fuwa continues from where it was.

One HTML file. No account, no internet, nothing leaves your device.

What you get

  • fuwa.html: the whole thing, one file, works offline.
  • Name it. Tap the name at the top to rename your Fuwa.
  • English and Japanese (EN / 日本語 toggle at the top right).
  • Tap the room to hug. Drop a star to feed. Or do nothing. Fuwa is fine.

Install (2 minutes)

  1. iPhone / iPad: save fuwa.html to Files, open it in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen, like a clock you leave on.
  2. Android / PC: open fuwa.html in Chrome → menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.

Your Fuwa's memory (name, how tame it is, stars eaten) is stored only on that device.
